# Credits and Scope

## Project Work

The Survivor is presented through concept and narrative development, interaction design, Blueprint implementation, asset development/refinement, scene assembly, music selection, sound editing and video presentation.

This archive concentrates on the project-specific Blueprint implementation. Assets developed with generative tools should be described with the relevant tool and the subsequent manual editing/assembly work when those assets are presented.

## Foundation

- Unreal Engine 5.7 — Epic Games.
- Unreal Engine VR template — starting point for the tracked pawn, controllers, baseline movement and grab systems.
- Elias Canetti, *Crowds and Power* — conceptual research reference.
